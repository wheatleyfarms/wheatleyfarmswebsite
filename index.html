What we do

We are constantly improving our pasture and looking for new and better techniques for raising our animals for the wellbeing of our customers.

Rotational Grazing

Rotational grazing is the act of moving our animals to fresh grass at least once a day. This gives the animals a clean environment every day. It also allows the land to recover by the next time the animals return to the same spot.

Small batch animal processing

We process our animals in small batches. That allows us to keep our areas clean and not overload the land with the waste products.

We don't believe in giving the animals anitbiotics and other medicinces

We don't want our customers consuming the medicines the animals do. Our breeding program selects the healthiest animals. Rotational grazing also keeps the animals in a healthy environment to reduce disease.
